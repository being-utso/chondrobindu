## Brand & Style

This design system draws its foundation from artisanal, tactile materiality—merging the mineral warmth of Mediterranean lime-washed plaster with the disciplined restraint of fermented indigo vat dyeing. It is grounded, scholarly, and humanistic, consciously avoiding synthetic tech-gloss, stark digital neons, and sci-fi tropes.

### Key Tenets
- **Artisanal Groundedness**: Every surface evokes physical substrate—chalk paper, pressed plaster, weathered stone, and plant-derived pigments.
- **Academic & Editorial Authority**: Clear typographical cadence, generous vertical cadence, and composed structures create an ambiance of thoughtful scholarship and deliberate documentation.
- **Organic Depth**: Hierarchy is communicated through pigment density, mineral surface tiers, and calibrated low-contrast borders rather than exaggerated, synthetic dropshadows.

## Colors

The palette establishes an organic dialogue between warm lime-washed mineral neutrals and rich natural dye extracts.

### Surface Hierarchy & Neutral Layers
- **Canvas / Background (`#EDEAE0`)**: Warm Lime-Washed Wall. Serves as the primary viewport canvas.
- **Surface Dim (`#E5E1D5`)**: Deepened stone wash for subtle spatial de-emphasis.
- **Surface Bright (`#F7F5F0`)**: Bright warm chalk paper wash.
- **Surface Container Lowest (`#F7F5F0`)**: Elevated cards and crisp content sheets mimicking heavy warm cotton paper.
- **Surface Container Low (`#E5E1D5`)**: Recessed warm stone wells, toolbars, and grouped content sections.
- **Surface Container (`#DFDACD`)**: Plaster tone for default structural panels.
- **Surface Container High (`#DCD7C8`)**: Pressed plaster tone used for card borders, dividers, and accentuated containers.
- **Surface Container Highest (`#D3CDC0`)**: Heavy mineral ground for hover interactions and highest-density structural segments.

### Pigment Accents & Functional Roles
- **Primary (`#2F4A6D`) / On-Primary (`#FFFFFF`)**: Natural Indigo Dye. Delivers focused authority for dominant action targets, key active states, and header accents. Primary container mirrors this density with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Secondary (`#A5673F`) / On-Secondary (`#FFFFFF`)**: Warm Rust. Used for secondary highlights, artisanal flags, and editorial annotations.
  - Secondary Container (`#F0E3D8`) / On-Secondary Container (`#5E3214`).
- **Tertiary (`#3B7A57`) / On-Tertiary (`#FFFFFF`)**: Organic Moss Green. Deployed for positive affirmations, success validation, and organic status indicators.
  - Tertiary Container (`#DCEEE3`) / On-Tertiary Container (`#174229`).
- **Error (`#B84A39`)**: Terracotta Crimson. Natural earthenware red for warnings, non-destructive alerts, and critical state feedback.

### Text & Ink Contrast
- **On-Surface (`#1F2A3A`)**: Deep Indigo Ink. Provides rich, non-harsh contrast for primary typography and titles.
- **On-Surface Variant (`#5A6578`)**: Weathered Slate. Provides legible secondary balance for captions, supporting text, and metadata.
- **Outline (`#DCD7C8`)**: Crisp pressed plaster perimeter line.
- **Outline Variant (`#E5E1D5`)**: Recessed, softer boundary rule.

## Typography

The typography relies entirely on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, tuned to an editorial, scholarly frequency. It balances geometric approachability with classic proportions.

### Guidelines
- **No Monospaced Technicalities**: Avoid cold data tables with monospaced digit paddings unless representing literal raw ledger feeds. Use proportional lining figures with clean tracking.
- **Rhythm & Line Height**: Maintain comfortable leading (minimum 1.5x on body text) to let indigo ink breathe across plaster surfaces.
- **Weights**: Standardize on Medium (500) and SemiBold (600) for structural accents. Reserve Bold (700) strictly for larger editorial titles.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an architectural grid with calm, generous pacing inspired by classic printed journals.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (>= 1280px)**: 12-column layout with `gutter-lg` (2rem) and `margin-lg` (4rem). Central content containers cap at 1200px.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column layout with `gutter` (1.5rem) and `margin-md` (2.5rem).
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with `gutter-sm` (1rem) and `margin` (1.5rem).

### Internal Rhythm
- Pair small elements (icons to labels) using `space-xs` and `space-sm`.
- Container interior paddings use `space-md` for compact trays, and `space-lg` for prominent chalk paper cards.
- Major section transitions leverage `space-xl` to maintain an uncluttered visual field.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial layering relies on physical surface shifts and low-contrast borders rather than dramatic dropshadows.

### Layering Philosophy
- **Physical Stacking**: Background `#EDEAE0` acts as the wall. Primary cards sit slightly raised on `#F7F5F0` (Chalk Paper), bordered by `#DCD7C8` (Pressed Plaster).
- **Borders & Insets**: Structural elements utilize a crisp 1px border (`#DCD7C8`). Inset panels and input wells recede into `#E5E1D5`.
- **Atmospheric Soft Shadow**: When absolute elevation is needed (e.g., floating dialogs or elevated popovers), apply an ultra-diffuse warm shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(31, 42, 58, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(31, 42, 58, 0.04);`
  - The shadow is tinted with `#1F2A3A` (Deep Indigo) to integrate seamlessly with the ink palette.

## Shapes

The design system uses soft, calibrated corners to reflect hand-pressed substrates, alongside fully rounded pills and circular avatars.

### Radius System
- **`rounded-full` (`9999px`)**: Reserved for circular avatars, status dots, interactive tags, and pill buttons.
- **`rounded-xl` (`12px` / `0.75rem`)**: The dedicated radius for primary content cards, elevated panels, and modal containers.
- **`rounded-lg` (`8px` / `0.5rem`)**: Applied to internal elements, input boxes, list item selections, and secondary inner containers.
- **`rounded-sm` (`4px` / `0.25rem`)**: Used for micro-badges, checkboxes, and inline alerts.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill**: Background `#2F4A6D` (Indigo), text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `9999px`. Height 44px, horizontal padding `1.5rem`. On hover, subtly shift background to `#253B57`.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Background `#E5E1D5`, text `#1F2A3A`, border 1px solid `#DCD7C8`, border-radius `9999px`. On hover, background shifts to `#DFDACD`.
- **Rust Accent Button**: Background `#A5673F` (Warm Rust), text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `9999px`.

### Cards
- **Elevated Card**: Background `#F7F5F0` (Warm Chalk Paper), border 1px solid `#DCD7C8` (Pressed Plaster), border-radius `12px` (`rounded-xl`). Internal padding `1.5rem` (`space-lg`).
- **Recessed Well**: Background `#E5E1D5`, border 1px solid `#DFDACD`, border-radius `8px` (`rounded-lg`).

### Form Inputs
- **Text Input**: Background `#F7F5F0`, 1px solid border `#DCD7C8`, border-radius `8px` (`rounded-lg`). Text in `#1F2A3A`, placeholder in `#5A6578`.
- **Focus State**: Border color transitions to `#2F4A6D` with a subtle outline glow: `0 0 0 2px rgba(47, 74, 109, 0.15)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills**: Border-radius `9999px`, padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`.
- **Indigo Tone**: Background `#2F4A6D`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Moss Tone**: Background `#DCEEE3`, text `#174229`.
- **Rust Tone**: Background `#F0E3D8`, text `#5E3214`.

### Avatars & Indicators
- **Avatars**: Circular with `border-radius: 9999px`, enclosed with a 1.5px border of `#DCD7C8` to delineate from `#EDEAE0` backgrounds.
- **Status Dots**: 8px or 10px circular dots (`rounded-full`), using `#3B7A57` for active and `#B84A39` for critical alerts.

### Lists & Dividers
- **Dividers**: 1px horizontal rule in `#DCD7C8`.
- **List Items**: Border-radius `8px` (`rounded-lg`) on hover with `#E5E1D5` background transitions.
